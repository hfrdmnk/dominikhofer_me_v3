## Design

The main goal for this iteration of the website was to create a minimal design that still has some personality and feels authentic to me.

My personal highlights in that regard are the variable accent colors (they change depending on the day you’re visiting this site), my signature and a custom quote at the bottom of every page and the personalized cards on [/races](/races).

During the process, I was inspired by these great designers and their websites. Pay them a visit too!

- [Alexander Sandberg](https://alexandersandberg.com/)
- [David Reina](https://davidreina.com/)
- [Benji Taylor](https://benji.org/)
- [Paco Coursey](https://paco.me/)
- [Glenn Hitchcock](https://glenn.me/)
- [Jakub Krehel](https://jakub.kr/)

The text is set in the beautiful [Newsreader](https://productiontype.com/font/newsreader) typeface for main headings and uses the system-ui font for everything else.

The website switches between light and dark mode automatically based on your OS’ settings.

The accent colors are inspired by the seven colors of the rainbow.

## Tech

When I started working on this version of the website, I also started dipping my toes into Golang. Naturally, I chose [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) as the framework that powers everything. All the content is managed with Markdown files, so no CMS is needed.

The [Standard.site](https://standard.site/) integration is handled by the phenomenal [Sequoia](https://sequoia.pub/) package by [Steve](https://stevedylan.dev/).

The [/now](/now) page is also being kept up to date via the [ATProtocol](https://atproto.com/): Books are synched directly from [Bookhive](https://bookhive.buzz/), the links I collect are taken from [Semble](https://semble.so).

The art for the cards I have on [/races](/races) are generated by a deterministic algorithm based on my real race data like my heart rate (parsed from the .fit file from that day).

The last played song feature is made possible by [Last.fm](https://www.last.fm/).
