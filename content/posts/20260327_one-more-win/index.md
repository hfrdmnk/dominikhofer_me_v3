If you’re watching only one video today, make it this one.

Not only is it exceptionally beautifully written, shot, color graded and edited, but it also has a very important message.

> All you need right now is a win.

Sometimes, whether or not we feel like we’re in a slump, the most important thing we can do is think about [the next step](/only-the-next-step-matters) to take.

And this step doesn’t even have to be big. Preferably, it should be a small one.

Because small wins are quick to achieve. Then you can go and find the next one. And stack them over time.

And before you know, you have built momentum.

I’ve experienced this countless times myself. This momentum that’s being built is usually the greatest predictor of whether or not I’m going to have [a great day or not](/the-good-and-the-bad-days).

So, just remember, the only thing you have to do is simple:

> You gotta ask yourself, what small win can I go and achieve, right now?
