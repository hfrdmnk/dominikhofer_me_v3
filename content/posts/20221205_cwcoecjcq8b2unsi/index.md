🤑

Pricing example:

We're looking at the price of 20 × 2'000-word blog posts:
(This is the cheapest Copy AI plan apart from the free one which would only be one blog post.)

http://copy.ai: $49/month

GPT-3 playground: $1.15 (one-time)

2/11

---

✖️

Calculation:

The prompt is roughly 150 words long.
Plus the 2'000 words output equals 2'150 words per post.
So 20 posts are 43'000 words.

Now, 750 words in the best GPT-3 model (Davinci) cost only 2 cents.

So let's calculate: 43'000 / 750 × \$0.02 = \$1.15

3/11

---

When signing up for OpenAI, you get $18 worth of free credits.

Meaning, you can generate:
\$18 / \$1.15 * 20 posts = ~313 blog posts for free!

4/11

---

Of course, you have to factor in some wasted credits for trial and error, but I think 250 blog posts would be realistic (this error margin would probably be a bit lower for Copy AI).

5/11

---

🤔

So bottom line is:

You can either generate 250 blog posts on your own for free,

Or

Pay $49 per month for 20 blog posts.
