Last night the clocks were moved forward by one hour.

The biggest consequence of this is usually one hour less of sleep. Not great.

But there’s another thing that’s happening today, and it’s a positive one: All of a sudden, the evenings are longer again.

More time to catch a few rays of sunlight on an afterwork run. 

And the possibility of seeing the sun set behind the [Jura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jura_Mountains) while I’m writing my daily blog posts.

Well, at least if the weather lets it happen. Not today, but maybe tomorrow.

I’m looking forward to these longer evenings.
