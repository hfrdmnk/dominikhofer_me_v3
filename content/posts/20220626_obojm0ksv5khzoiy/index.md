We live in a time full of opportunities: The permissionless area.

But how should you leverage them?

@jackbutcher has created what is probably the most comprehensive guide to answer this question.

Here are my 5 key takeaways &amp; opinions on “The Permissionless Apprentice” 👇

---

@jackbutcher 1. A new world

You have to acknowledge one fact: The internet is ginormous. 

And every day, there are more people on it = a big opportunity.

This also means that the bar for creating value is incredibly low. It's easy to exceed expectations.

Use this to your advantage.

---

@jackbutcher 2. Your uniqueness

Show the world your uniqueness – your interests and abilities.
Build your personal monopoly around it (h/t @david_perell).

You're essentially able to invent your own work, which is awesome! And you don't need anyone's permission to do that.

---

@jackbutcher @david_perell 3. Show – don't tell

Having a portfolio where you consistently show your work is the single most valuable asset you can build.

One of the best places to publish what you do is Twitter (“public résume). But you can also use your personal Website, YouTube, or any other Platform.

---

@jackbutcher @david_perell 4. Do the work beforehand

It's easily possible to connect with everyone on the internet, including the people you look up to.

But do the work before reaching out to them. Show, what you're capable of.

Craft an offer they can't refuse.

---

@jackbutcher @david_perell 5. Live in the future…

…and build what's missing. This is the key part.

Get creative, and think about what you would want to see existing in this world. Then go build it.

And show it to the internet.
