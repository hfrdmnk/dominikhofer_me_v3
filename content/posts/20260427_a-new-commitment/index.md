A couple of weeks back, I finished my first [30-day PACT](/writing-pact). During this time, [I wrote and published one post every single day](/30-days-of-writing).

Whilst I didn't continue with the daily posts, I really enjoyed the momentum it gave me. That's what I want to replicate now, but in another area of my life.

In February, [I wrote about an exciting new project I started working on](https://buttondown.com/dominikhofer/archive/a-new-day-begins/) called “[Morgenblau](https://morgen.blue)”. Now it's April and… not much has happened since then, honestly. Because [a lot has been going on in life](/welcome-naida).

But I'm still very excited about the project, so I want to get into the flow again.

That's why I'm starting another 30-day PACT:

> I will commit daily to the [Morgenblau repo](https://tangled.org/dominik.social/morgenblau) and post about it on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/dominik.social) for the next 30 days.

You can also follow along on this website by checking out the posts with [#morgenblau](/tag/morgenblau).

I already marked day 1 off for this challenge, because [my post](https://bsky.app/profile/dominik.social/post/3mkidwnadlc2e) for the day is out.

See you tomorrow on Bluesky!
