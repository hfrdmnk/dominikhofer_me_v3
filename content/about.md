## Who

My name is Dominik and I‘m a 25 years old guy from Switzerland, living near Bern.

I‘m still trying to figure out what exactly I want to do with my life (and by extension, who I truly am). And this website serves as a time capsule of this process. For me, but also for you. Because the journey is way more important than the destination.

I consider myself a very curious person with many interests. Right now, I‘m into:

- Coding (in general)
- Laravel, React & Go (in particular)
- The world of personal websites (aka the IndieWeb)
- The AT Protocol
- Blogging
- Running
- Reading
- Living a simpler life

I suppose [my blog](/posts) and [/now](/now) are pretty good places to check and see, what‘s on top of my mind recently.

## What

By day, I’m working as the Lead Engineer @[Trueform](https://trueform.agency) where I'm currently building [Billow](https://billow.so). Apart from that, I create my own things at night. Mostly by designing & coding stuff, but I also run regularly and I'm trying to get better at articulating my thoughts (aka writing on [this blog](/posts)).

Other than that, I like to learn and understand as much as I can about this fascinating world we’re living in. Mostly by myself and from books or the internet. But because that can get a bit lonely, and you have to be very disciplined to study the hard parts, I'm also attending university – currently studying computer science @[University of Bern](https://unibe.ch). My minors are maths and social sciences.

For a more up-to-date overview of what I’m doing, take a look at the [/now](/now) page.

## Personal Values

I consider these my core values that I try to align my lifestyle with:

**Autonomy**, because I am capable of taking responsibility and take my life into my own hands.

**Calmness**, because a calm mind is more present and a calm person is more pleasant to be around.

**Creativity**, because it's a privilege to be able to make the world a better place through the things we create and the ideas we share.

**Knowledge**, because education is what's left, after no dollar remains.

**Sustainability**, because long-term thinking always triumphs over short-term quick wins with no lasting effect.

They are not set in stone and might change in the future, but right now, they just feel right.

## Why

This website serves multiple purposes:

- It allows me to put myself out there and meet great people.
- It helps me get to know myself better (Writing the copy for this page is really hard).
- It documents my life.
- It lets me work on my craft (design, code, writing) and share it.
- It‘s a way better resume than LinkedIn.

A personal website is a great thing to have. It‘s my ever-evolving project and allows me to tinker around on my own terms.
